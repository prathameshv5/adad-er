# Primary Use Cases

## PwD User Journey
1. **Registration**: Create account with disability details and UDID verification
2. **Profile Setup**: Add disability categories and support requirements
3. **Product Discovery**: Browse products filtered by disability subcategories
4. **Interest Expression**: Mark products of interest for future reference
5. **Review & Feedback**: Rate and review used products
6. **Community Engagement**: Read success stories and engage with content
7. **Help Access**: Access role-specific help articles and support

## Vendor Workflow
1. **Business Registration**: Submit company details and business license
2. **Profile Verification**: Admin approval of vendor credentials
3. **Product Listing**: Add products with disability subcategory mapping
4. **Inventory Management**: Update stock status and pricing
5. **Customer Interaction**: Respond to product reviews and inquiries
6. **Analytics Access**: Track product interest and review metrics

## Admin Operations
1. **User Management**: Approve vendor and employer registrations
2. **Content Moderation**: Review and approve product listings and reviews
3. **Story Creation**: Publish inspirational success stories
4. **Help Management**: Create and maintain help articles
5. **System Monitoring**: Track user engagement and platform health
6. **Notification Management**: Send system-wide announcements

## Caregiver Support
1. **PwD Association**: Link to persons with disabilities under care
2. **Proxy Operations**: Assist with product discovery and reviews
3. **Support Coordination**: Help with product selection and usage
4. **Communication**: Interface with vendors and support systems

## Donor & Employer Integration
1. **Profile Creation**: Register organizational details
2. **Opportunity Posting**: List donation opportunities or job openings
3. **Matching**: Connect with relevant PwD users
4. **Impact Tracking**: Monitor contribution effectiveness

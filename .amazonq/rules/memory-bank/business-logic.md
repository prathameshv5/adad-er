# Business Logic & Workflows

## User Registration & Profile Management
- Multi-role registration with role-specific data collection
- Document verification for vendors (business license) and PwD (UDID)
- Profile status management with approval workflows
- Caregiver-PwD relationship establishment

## Product Management Workflow
- Vendor product listing with disability subcategory mapping
- Admin review and approval process with rejection reasons
- Stock status and pricing management
- Media attachment (images, demo videos)

## Discovery & Matching System
- Disability-based product filtering
- PwD interest expression and tracking
- Notification system for relevant products
- Review-based product validation

## Review & Feedback Ecosystem
- PwD product reviews with rating system
- Vendor and admin reply capabilities
- Review moderation with status management
- Community-driven product validation

## Support & Help System
- Role-based help content (vendor vs PwD specific)
- Action tracking for help effectiveness
- Support request escalation
- Knowledge base management

## Community Features
- Admin-curated success stories
- PwD engagement through story likes
- Notification system for community updates
- User interaction tracking
